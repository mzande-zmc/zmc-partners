Meet the Founder

Michael Zande

Founder & Principal Consultant

For more than 18 years, I have worked inside complex manufacturing environments helping organizations improve performance through stronger leadership systems, organizational alignment, and operational consistency.

I started as a plant HR leader — working directly with supervisors, managers, plant leaders, and associates to solve real operational problems tied to execution.

Not theory — daily operations.

Later, I moved into enterprise and global leadership roles, helping design the larger systems behind performance: organizational structure, succession planning, leadership standards, talent strategy, workforce planning, and execution across multi-site operations.

I have supported plant leaders, senior executives, and global leadership teams across 34 sites in 15 countries, helping connect leadership decisions to measurable operational results.

This work has always centered on the same question:

That is the work behind ZMC.

Why ZMC exists

Why ZMC Exists

Most leadership development fails for one reason: The issue is not training. The issue is execution.

Observed

What I kept seeing:

  • Strong training, little lasting behavior change
  • Supervisors returning to old habits
  • Inconsistent manager coaching
  • Leadership standards changing by shift or by leader
  • Recurring operational problems that never fully improved
What works

What actually creates change:

  • Clear leadership expectations
  • Skill development tied to real work
  • Coaching and reinforcement
  • Peer learning and accountability
  • Structured execution rhythms

The results that proved this approach are documented on the homepage — including a 36% reduction in absenteeism within six months and a facility vision that had been stalled for years finally moving forward.

Most companies do not need more leadership training.

They need a leadership operating system.

This work is built for organizations where leadership execution is a business issue — not just a training issue.

The name

Why "Partners" Matters

ZMC Partners is intentionally named.

While I lead the work as Founder and Principal Consultant, this business was never built alone.

"Partners" reflects the people who make this work possible — my wife and our children — who provide the support, foundation, and purpose behind everything we are building.

It also reflects how I believe meaningful work should happen: through service, collaboration, and responsibility.

Strong businesses are built when people contribute in ways that create lasting value — for operations, for families, and for the communities those businesses support. This business exists to create that kind of impact.

This is technically a solo business, but never a solo effort.

That is why we are ZMC Partners.

Leadership systems for manufacturing — defining and executing consistent leadership across supervisors, managers, and plant leadership.
